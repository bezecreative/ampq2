Payment Health

The Members Who Didn’t Choose to Leave.

Why we focused on payment health this quarter

Some member cancellations were never a choice: a recharge payment quietly failed and the member churned without ever knowing. The good news, most of these payment failures are preventable or recoverable through a variety of tactics. Drawing on millions of membership recharges across the AMP portfolio, here’s where the money actually leaks, and how it can be clawed back.

How payment failure and recovery works

Prevent failures Up-to-date card credentials and smart acceptance logic prevent approximately 1 out of 3 potential payment failures
Payment fails Ultimately, about 1 out of 11 initial recharge payments fail and move into recovery
Retries Roughly half of failed payments are recovered through a payment retry schedule
Dunning Automated payment recovery. The member is nudged by email and text to update their payment method, recovering about 1 of 5 failed payments
Win-back If nothing works, the member is invited to reactivate later

InsightSign-up promotions are a common way of attracting new members; while they drive higher sales, they can lead to higher churn — both voluntary and through failed payments. This is because the higher the recharge amount, the higher the chance of payment failure, all else equal.

Failed Payment Recovery Strategies

Failed payments can be recovered through multiple strategies. Email and text dunning alert the member that their payment failed and provide an easy process of updating their card. Retries re-attempt the charge on a schedule, typically over 14 days.

Most recovery happens in the first 7 days

Insight

Optimize your retry schedule to maximize results

Retrying at the same time each day leaves recovery on the table. Smarter schedules vary the timing and dates by card type and failure reason, hitting each payment when it’s most likely to clear, to increase retry success while optimizing retry costs.

How Operators Can Act on This

The numbers above only matter if they change what happens at the wash. Four moves, in priority order.

Activate failure prevention with your processor.Network tokens, automatic card account updates, and real-time acceptance optimization stop as many false declines as possible before they ever reach recovery.

Turn on recovery.Email and text dunning plus an optimized retry schedule catch the failures that slip through, including the expired and replaced cards retries alone can’t fix.

Capture verified contact info at signup.A confirmed email and phone number are what let dunning reach the member. A missing phone number is one of the top reasons outreach gets skipped today.

Target win-backs by cancellation reason.Failed-payment and other circumstantial exits return at the highest rates. Reach them first, and where a full membership won’t land, a retail wash loyalty program can.
